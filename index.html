03 / The playbook

How to
win well.

No theatre. Just useful work, clearly told.

01

Choose a real user

Make your audience specific enough to call by name. Empathy is a competitive advantage.

02

Ship the smallest proof

A working slice beats a perfect deck. Show us the moment your idea clicks.

03

Use what is already here

Company data, APIs, and internal tools are fair game. Ask owners early; respect privacy always.

04

Tell the honest story

Demo what works, name what does not, and make your next step impossible to misunderstand.
